💡 How to Use SnipSnap

SnipSnap is designed for intuitive, lightning-fast workflows:

Taking a Screenshot: Click the SnipSnap menu bar icon and select "Capture Region," then drag to select any part of your screen. Release to capture instantly.

Pinning a Screenshot: After capture, choose "Pin to Screen" from the preview window. The screenshot will stay visible on top of all your apps. Drag it anywhere, resize it, or unpin with the ✕ button.

Extracting Text (OCR): Open any image or screenshot in SnipSnap, click the "OCR" button (or press the shortcut). Recognized text will pop up instantly, ready to copy.

Translating Text: Highlight any text in a document, browser, or any app. Press your translation shortcut (default: Cmd+Shift+T). A neat inline bubble shows the translation, in true Safari style.

SnipSnap is compatible with macOS 11.0 (Big Sur) and later versions, including Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma, and the latest releases.

Absolutely! That's the beauty of SnipSnap. All text recognition runs locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your data never leaves your Mac.
